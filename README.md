Sizzle Street — Rush Hour Kitchen: a fast browser cooking game. Run a diner kitchen through a 150-second rush — take the tickets on the order board, grab ingredients from the front counter, chop, grill and fry (careful: done food burns), build each dish on a plate at the assembly counter and serve it at the hatch before the customer's patience runs out. Tips follow the patience left, combos multiply the take, and three walked-out tickets end the shift. Played with WASD/arrows + E, or by clicking counters to walk to them. All art and sound are drawn and synthesized in code — no assets, no network.
